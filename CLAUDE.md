## Custom Sections
These are custom-built sections specific to this store:

- `sections/fz-about.liquid` — About Us + Custom Orders section
- `sections/fz-custom-design.liquid` — Standalone Custom Design CTA section
- `sections/fz-countdown.liquid` — Countdown timer for drops

## Notes
- Theme is based on a Shopify 2.0 architecture (sections + blocks)
- Custom styles are scoped inside `{% stylesheet %}` tags within each section
- Fonts used: Fredoka (headings), Nunito (body)
